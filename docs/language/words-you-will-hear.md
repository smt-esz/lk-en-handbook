# Words you will hear

This handbook is written in English throughout. School, however, runs in German, and on an examination day nobody will hand you a "fair copy" sheet. This is the one page where both languages sit side by side, so that you can match what you read here to what you are told in the corridor.

!!! tip "At a glance"

    |   |   |
    |---|---|
    | **Purpose** | Match the English terms used in this handbook to the official German ones you will actually hear |
    | **Structure** | Grouped by where you meet the word: the course, assessment, the examination day |
    | **Register & voice** | Official terminology. None of it belongs in an English text you write |
    | **Length** | One page. It is a lookup, not a reading task |
    | **Watch out** | Never use the German terms in your own English writing. That is exactly what the mediation task penalizes |

## The course

| You will hear | In this handbook | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Leistungskurs, LK | Advanced Course | Your main subject, five lessons a week, examined in writing |
| Grundkurs, GK | Basic Course | A subject taken at the standard level |
| Jahrgangsstufe 11 / 12 | Year 11 / Year 12 | The two years of the upper school |
| Kurshalbjahr, 11/I | Term, first term of Year 11 | One of the four half-years that count towards your final grade |
| Fachkonferenz | English department | The teachers of a subject, who decide numbers and weightings |
| Oberstufenberater | Sixth-form coordinator | The teacher who advises you on subject choices and the examination |

## Assessment

| You will hear | In this handbook | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Klausur | Class exam | A long written exam in class, up to 180 minutes |
| Sonstige Noten | Other assessment | Everything that is not a class exam: oral work, tests, presentations |
| Komplexe Leistung | Project assessment | A project you plan, carry out, document and present |
| Notenpunkte | Grade points | The 0 to 15 scale that replaces school grades in the upper school |
| Bewertungseinheiten, BE | Assessment units | A points system used in other subjects. **English does not use it** |
| Sprachmittlung | Mediation | Transferring content from German into English for a given addressee |
| Ganzschrift | Set text | A complete book read in class, as opposed to an extract |

## The examination

| You will hear | In this handbook | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Abitur | Final examination | The school-leaving examination at the end of Year 12 |
| Vorabitur | Mock examination | A class exam written under full examination conditions |
| Praktischer Prüfungsteil | Speaking exam | The paired oral exam, worth 20 % of your English grade |
| Reinschrift | Fair copy | The white sheets that are actually marked. One side only |
| Konzept | Draft | The grey sheets for planning. Not marked unless you say so |
| Chiffre | School code | The number identifying your school, written top left |
| Kennzahl | Candidate number | The number identifying you, written top right. Your name never appears |
| Mantelbogen | Cover sheet | Records how many sheets you handed in |
| Einsichtnahme | Viewing session | The appointment at which you may look at your marked script |
| Nachteilsausgleich | Access arrangements | Adjustments granted on application, for example extra time |
| Täuschungsversuch | Attempted deception | Anything from a hidden note to a dictionary that breaks the rules |

## Marking

| You will hear | In this handbook | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Erstkorrektur | First marking | Done by your own teacher |
| Zweitkorrektur | Second marking | Done anonymously by a teacher from another school |
| Drittkorrektur | Third marking | Only if the first two disagree. Sets the final result |
| Gutachten | Marking report | The written justification of a mark |

## One warning

Every word in the left-hand column is a **German** word. None of them belongs in an English text you hand in, not even in quotation marks, unless the task explicitly asks you to explain a German institution to an English-speaking reader. That is the one case where you name it and then gloss it:

> the *Abitur*, the German school-leaving examination that qualifies students for university
