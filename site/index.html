    English LK Handbook

    Good to have you here. This is your handbook for the English Advanced Course, and it exists so that you never have to guess. Whatever you are working on, a mediation, a cartoon analysis, a speech, the rules are written down somewhere on this site, in the shortest form that still helps.

    You do not have to read it front to back. Come in when you are stuck, take what you need, go back to your text. The search bar at the top is usually faster than clicking.

    How the pages work

    Every how-to page follows the same pattern, so you always know where to look:

    Part What you get
    At a glance The rules in about thirty seconds
    Step by step The long version, in the order you actually work
    Useful phrases Sentence starters you can take straight into your text
    Checklist Tick it off as you go. Your progress is remembered on this device
    Common mistakes The ones that really turn up in class exams, so you can avoid them

    The At a glance box always answers the same five questions in the same order, so you can read it without hunting:

    Line The question it answers
    Purpose What is this text supposed to do to its reader?
    Structure What skeleton does a marker expect to find?
    Register & voice How formal, which person, which tense?
    Length How long, and does the task say?
    Watch out What is the one mistake that costs most here?

    On the exam pages the same box answers Format, Timing, What counts and Watch out, because those are the questions that matter there.

    Good to know

    This site works offline once you have opened it, so a weak connection in the classroom is not a problem. On your phone or iPad you can add it to your home screen and it behaves like an app.

    Everything here is written in English, including the terms for things school will name in German. If you are ever unsure which English word matches what you just heard in class, Words you will hear puts both side by side.

    Something missing, unclear or plain wrong? Say so and it gets fixed.
